Nataco

A design studio with an illustrated world of its own.

From the archive

First repository commit: 23 February 2020. The Dyeus → Nataco rename is dated 24 June 2021.

Nataco’s site brought custom illustration, expressive typography and a playful 3D scene into a design-and-development studio website. Its pages covered web applications, Shopify work and design subscriptions.

The repository starts under the Dyeus name in February 2020. A June 2021 commit renames the site Nataco, giving the archive a clear record of that transition.

The original homepage, service-page artwork and animated hero survive. The film below is the recovered hero animation; the gallery shows the site and its illustration system.
