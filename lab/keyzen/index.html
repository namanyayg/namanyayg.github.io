October 2013 is the date recorded in the existing portfolio.

I was obsessed with improving my typing speed. My reasoning was simple: faster typing meant faster programming. So I made KeyZen in a few hours at a hackathon.

I especially enjoyed making the pastel keyboard and animating the keys. The game won first prize that day.
