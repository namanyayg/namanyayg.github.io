Webstiles

A way to sketch a website’s visual language directly in the browser.

From the archive

Open Webstiles ↗Source ↗

Webstiles brought colors, typography and interface elements together in a browser-based design sheet. It helped clients compare directions before a full website was built.

The early portfolio describes Webstiles as part of the client workflow and records that its responsive template was built on Sympl.
