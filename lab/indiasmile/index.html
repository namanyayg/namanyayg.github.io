IndiaSmile

COVID-19 information alongside ways to help people through isolation.

From the archive

June 2020 is the date recorded in the existing portfolio.

Visit IndiaSmile ↗

IndiaSmile combined pandemic statistics with a community and resources for staying informed. Its purpose extended from tracking numbers to finding ways to stay connected during lockdown.

The original portfolio describes a WhatsApp and Telegram community of more than 2,500 members and the challenge of adapting the backend as traffic grew.
