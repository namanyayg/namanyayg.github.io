Explore Clas ↗Source ↗

Clas gave a small website the feel of an application: click a section and the old panel fades into the next. The original project also came with two tutorials explaining how it worked.

The restored demo keeps its early-web character, with repaired navigation and a contact form that stays inside the exhibit.
